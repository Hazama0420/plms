# Mobile UX Audit

Check each page for:

## Content
- Is the primary purpose obvious in 3 seconds?
- Is the most important information above the fold?
- Are secondary details hidden until needed?

## Layout
- No horizontal overflow
- Comfortable side padding
- Clear section separation
- Predictable vertical rhythm

## Interaction
- Touch targets are comfortable
- Filters do not overload the screen
- CTA remains discoverable
- Navigation is scan-friendly

## Property
- Image remains dominant
- Price is easy to find
- Location is easy to find
- 2–4 specs are enough on cards

## Visual
- Emerald remains recognizable
- No green overload
- Typography hierarchy is strong
- Motion is subtle
